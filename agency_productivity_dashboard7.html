📖 Plain-English Operational Glossary

What do these metrics mean in simple terms?

Friction Index (1.70x)

The ratio of logged hours to deliverable estimates. 1.0x is perfect parity. 1.70x means your agency consumes 102 minutes of staff time to produce 60 minutes of client deliverable work.

Scope Creep (37.5h)

Free favors done for clients that were never contracted. Instead of issuing formal change orders, account managers absorbed 37.5 hours of unbilled work as "goodwill".

Stealth Overhead (272.5h)

Meetings (218.5h) and unbilled speculative pitches (54h). This time is invisible on deliverable charts, but it destroys daylight hours, forcing creatives to work at night.

Dependency Idle Time (28h)

"Hurry up and wait." Staff spend days waiting for delayed client feedback or missing copy, then work 16-hour weekend shifts when the client demands a rushed delivery.
